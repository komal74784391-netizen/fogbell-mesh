# Derivation table — 2021 Interim Ordinance to 2026 Code

This table records where provisions of the Interim Information Disclosure
Ordinance (2021) were carried into the Consolidated Information Stewardship
Code (2026 Revision). It is maintained by the Compliance Council secretariat.

| 2021 Ordinance provision | 2026 Code provision | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Article 12, paragraph 1 | Article 18, paragraph 1 | Carried over without any change in obligation |
| Article 31, paragraph 2 | Article 47 | Substantively amended; the notification period and the associated transfer-statement duties were revised |
| Article 44, paragraph 3 | Article 52 | Renumbered only; wording unchanged |

A provision marked as carried over or renumbered only is not a substantive
change and is outside the scope of a revision map.
