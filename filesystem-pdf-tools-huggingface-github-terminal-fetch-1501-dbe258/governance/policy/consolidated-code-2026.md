# Consolidated Information Stewardship Code (2026 Revision)

Adopted by the Northbeam Compliance Council on 12 January 2026.
Effective 1 March 2026.

This Code supersedes the Interim Information Disclosure Ordinance (2021).
Where a provision of the 2021 ordinance is not restated in this Code, that
provision lapses on the effective date.

## Part I — General Provisions

### Article 2. Purpose and construction

This Code establishes minimum controls for the stewardship of regulated
information held by controllers and processors within the jurisdiction.

A reference in this Code to a Part, Article or paragraph is a reference to a
Part, Article or paragraph of this Code unless the context requires otherwise.

In construing this Code, a provision that restates a requirement of the 2021
ordinance without altering its obligation does not create a new obligation.

### Article 4. Scope of regulated information

Regulated information means any record, file, dataset or derived product that
identifies or relates to an individual, a household or a client of a controller.

Regulated information does not include aggregate statistics that cannot be
related back to an individual or household.

A controller that holds regulated information remains responsible for it even
where processing is delegated to a processor.

## Part II — Records and Disclosure

### Article 11. Disclosure ledger

Each controller shall maintain a disclosure ledger recording every disclosure
of regulated information made to a third party.

The ledger shall record the date of the disclosure, the recipient, the purpose
and the categories of information disclosed.

The ledger shall be maintained in a form that permits inspection without
reconstruction from other systems.

### Article 18. Disclosure record retention

Each controller shall retain a written record of every regulated information disclosure for a period of not less than five years from the date of the disclosure.

The record shall identify the recipient, the purpose of the disclosure, and the categories of regulated information disclosed.

Records retained under this Article shall be produced to the Supervisory Office on request within ten (10) business days.

### Article 23. Individual access requests

A controller shall respond to an access request from an individual within
forty-five (45) days of receiving the request.

Where an access request is refused, the controller shall record the ground of
refusal and shall inform the individual of the right to seek review.

## Part III — Processors and Transfers

### Article 29. Processor due diligence

Before engaging a processor, a controller shall assess the processor's
information security controls and its capacity to comply with this Code.

The assessment shall be documented and retained for the duration of the
engagement and for five years afterwards.

A controller shall review the assessment at least once every two years.

### Article 36. Transfer impact statements

Where regulated information is transferred outside the jurisdiction, the
controller shall prepare a transfer impact statement before the transfer
commences.

The statement shall describe the safeguards adopted, the categories of
information transferred and the identity of the receiving entity.

### Article 41. Processor register

A controller shall maintain a register of all processors engaged by the
controller.

The register shall identify each processor, the categories of regulated
information handled and the location of processing facilities.

The register shall be updated within five (5) business days of any change to a
processor engagement.

## Part IV — Supervision and Notification

### Article 45. Supervisory notifications

A controller shall notify the Supervisory Office of a reportable incident
within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the incident.

The notification shall describe the nature of the incident, the categories of
regulated information affected and the measures taken in response.

### Article 47. Processor onboarding notification

This Article applies to every controller that engages a third-party processor to handle regulated information, whether the processor operates inside or outside the controller's jurisdiction.

The controller shall notify the Supervisory Office of any processor onboarding event within ten (10) business days of the effective date of that event, and shall include in the notification the identity of the processor, the categories of regulated information involved, and the location of processing facilities.

Where a notification under this Article discloses a transfer of regulated information outside the jurisdiction, the controller shall also file a transfer impact statement describing the safeguards adopted, and shall retain a copy of that statement for not less than five years.

### Article 48. Corrective action

Where the Supervisory Office finds a contravention of this Code, the controller
shall submit a corrective action plan within thirty (30) days of the finding.

The plan shall set out the steps to be taken, the responsible officer and the
date by which each step will be completed.

### Article 52. Penalties

A contravention of this Code may attract a civil penalty in accordance with the
schedule published by the Supervisory Office.

The schedule shall be reviewed by the Compliance Council every three years.
