# Inspection Record 2026-Q1-07

Issuer: Office of Records and Disclosure Oversight
Subject: Northbeam Analytics — disclosure controls and processor notifications
Date: 2026-02-18

## Finding 1 — Processor onboarding notice period

The inspection tested notifications made after processor onboarding events.
The requirement applied to the sampled events was stated in the Interim
Information Disclosure Ordinance (2021), Article 31, paragraph 2 as:

"The controller shall notify the Supervisory Office of any processor onboarding event within thirty (30) days of the effective date of that event, and shall include in the notification the identity of the processor, the categories of regulated information involved, and the location of processing facilities."

The corresponding provision of the Consolidated Information Stewardship Code
(2026 Revision) governs remediation for this finding. The notice period and the
notification contents must be restated using the 2026 text.

## Finding 2 — Disclosure record retention

The inspector also sampled disclosure records. The applicable requirement was
stated in the Interim Information Disclosure Ordinance (2021), Article 12,
paragraph 1 as:

"Each controller shall retain a written record of every regulated information disclosure for a period of not less than five years from the date of the disclosure."

The corresponding provision of the 2026 Code restates this requirement without
any change in obligation. No remediation action is required for this finding.

## Inspector note

The review period ran from 1 October 2025 to 31 December 2025. Records were
sampled from the disclosure ledger and the processor register. The findings
above are the only matters carried forward to the remediation log.
